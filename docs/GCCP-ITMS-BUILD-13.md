# GCCP-ITMS-BUILD-13 — Assurance & Controls

## Scope

- Item Group-first physical asset and custody verification.
- Verified, missing, custody-mismatch and damaged outcomes.
- Exception register derived from verification results.
- Controlled retirement, disposal and write-off requests.
- Sequential IT Asset Manager and IT Head approval.
- Asset moves to disposal-pending when requested and disposed/written-off only after final approval.
- Verification and disposal events are retained in the Asset Register lifecycle timeline.

## Validation boundary

This is a localhost-only control foundation. Evidence attachments, formal exception closure and disposal certificates can be extended in subsequent checkpoints.
