# GCCP-ITMS-BUILD-16 — Asset Retirement & Circularity

Status: Built and locked

## Scope

- Separate controlled routes for scrap/recycling and donation/CSR/giveaway.
- Multi-item-group selection followed by single or bulk asset selection.
- One controlled batch reference, approval chain, handover and declaration can cover multiple assets.
- Every asset in the batch remains individually reserved, status-controlled and traceable.
- Only unallocated, non-retired assets may enter a request.
- IT Asset Manager and IT Head approval stages.
- Physical handover remains a separate completion control after approval.
- Assets are reserved from allocation while a request is active.
- Completed assets become permanently unavailable and retain their identity/history.
- Cancelled requests restore the asset's prior lifecycle state.
- Data-sanitisation/non-applicability confirmation, recipient/recycler details, handover reference, value and justification are retained.
- Route-specific printable declaration with a numbered asset schedule and accountable signature blocks.

## Important boundary

Generated declarations are controlled internal lifecycle records. They do not claim tax, CSR, accredited recycling or statutory certification. External certificates and evidence can be linked when document storage is connected.
