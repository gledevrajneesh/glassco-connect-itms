# GCCP-ITMS-BUILD-16 — Notifications & Email Workflow

- Header notification centre with unread count, read state and direct module navigation.
- Live alerts for approvals, maintenance due/overdue, open incidents, lifecycle signatures, disposal and warranty expiry.

- Email history shows recipient, preparation status and timestamp.
- No credentials, paid Firebase functions or paid delivery services are used in the localhost/free-plan foundation.
