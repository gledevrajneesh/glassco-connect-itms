# GCCP-ITMS-BUILD-10 — Asset Identity & QR Labels

- Standards-compliant QR generated for every registered asset.
- QR contains only the stable controlled asset-profile URL.
- No employee, cost, credential or sensitive custody data is encoded.
- Single, filtered bulk and category-wise label selection.
- Compact 50 × 25 mm and standard 70 × 40 mm print formats.
- Initial prints, damaged/lost replacements and bulk reprints retain audit history.
