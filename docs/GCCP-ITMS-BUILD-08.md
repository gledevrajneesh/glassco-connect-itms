# GCCP-ITMS-BUILD-08 — Employee Onboarding & Offboarding

- Department-first employee selection.
- Onboarding form supports one or multiple in-stock IT assets.
- Offboarding automatically loads every asset in current employee custody.
- Approved allocation history is the authoritative custody source; stock-status inconsistencies do not hide allocated assets.
- Completion closes active allocation and transfer-derived custody links for the offboarded employee.
- Offboarding requires a controlled return disposition: In stock, Under repair or Scrap.
- Offboarding explicitly asks whether User Master should be deactivated; the decision is retained and printed.
- Employee and IT Manager sign-off states are retained.
- Completed offboarding deactivates the employee and sends returned assets to inspection pending.
- Professional A4 sign-off form can be printed or saved as PDF and emailed to the employee.
